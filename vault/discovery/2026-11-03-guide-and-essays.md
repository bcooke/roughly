# Guide and essay publication

Roughly needs both maintained explanations and room for arguments that develop over time. A Guide is the initial public home for definitions, interpreting results, and the ideas behind the project. About remains a short description of the project as it stands. The repository vault retains deeper linked research and technical contracts; it is not automatically published wholesale.

## Forms and ownership

| Form | Purpose | Time policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| About | What Roughly is, what it offers, and its open purpose | Maintained present-tense explanation |
| Guide | Definitions, worked examples, methods, sources, and participation/contribution guidance as capabilities develop | Evergreen, revised when meaning or behavior changes |
| Essays or blog | Arguments, intellectual history, experiments, and project history | Dated, attributed writing with corrections and links to superseding work |
| Vault | Research evidence, alternatives, domain contracts, and implementation records | Atomic notes plus clearly dated research and decisions |

The first guide includes an orientation, a percentage-definition page, a knowledge-and-evidence page, and a grouped reading map. It distinguishes proposed vocabulary from implemented features. A source link supports the associated claim; an entire book is not claimed as read on the strength of one passage.

## Possible introductory essay series

1. **What the world's percentages could contain** — experiences, circumstances, actions, and preferences; what is worth collecting and why.
2. **A definition is not a measurement** — knowledge, empirical targets, language, and evidence, with disagreements between traditions preserved.
3. **When a crowd helps** — Surowiecki and collective estimation, self-report, independence, and selection.
4. **What a percentage leaves out** — populations, missingness, uncertainty, framing, and changing instruments.
5. **Building an open body of evidence** — traceability, corrections, source reuse, privacy, and contribution.

These are editorial proposals, not published posts or first-person writing attributed to a contributor. Personal history would require a separately authored historical piece; it does not belong in the evergreen About page. The [research review](2026-11-03-knowledge-and-measurement.md) and [Population Proportions](../Population%20Proportions.md) supply starting material.

## Next editorial checks

Exercise definitions against concrete question families before adopting a permanent branded noun. Expand beyond the first review's traditions, deepen the most relevant sources, and invite correction. Contribution guidance should describe real available workflows rather than imply that public poll creation, data submission, or research APIs are already open.
