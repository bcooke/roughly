# Writing and question links

## Possibility

Let a writer connect a statement, tentative assumption, or open question to the relevant evidence in Roughly. A reader could inspect the exact question and its responses, or intentionally enter an answering experience when one exists. Different intentions need different destinations; a claim about results is not the same as an invitation to answer.

## Contracts to investigate

- Search and suggest relevant instruments without treating similar wording as equivalent evidence.
- Let the author inspect the exact wording, source, population, denominator, and cutoff before accepting a link.
- Distinguish a changing live result from a versioned citation. A link to an open poll cannot substantiate “most people” by itself.
- Preserve an answering path's exposure context rather than assuming readers arriving from an article are unprimed.

A repository skill, editor integration, browser extension, or MCP interface could provide authoring assistance. No integration mechanism is selected. Start with usable stable links and a concrete writing workflow before adding another service. Author-specific configuration belongs with the writing environment; a reusable public contract belongs here.
