# Question imagery and illustration

## Possibility

Images or a consistent illustration style might help people recognize a question's subject, clarify an unfamiliar object, and make browsing more engaging. Generated illustrations could provide a coherent treatment across many questions. Functional icons, explanatory reference images, topic illustrations, and photographs are separate candidates.

An image that is part of the question itself also differs from decoration around it. A visual-choice question needs its exact images preserved as instrument content. A browsing illustration might still affect who opens the question or how they interpret it.

## Uncertainty

Imagery might clarify meaning, prime an interpretation, suggest an answer, influence participation, or have little effect. The direction and size cannot be assumed for Roughly. A common style can reduce some variation without establishing neutrality; generated imagery can introduce errors and associations of its own.

More participation is useful only in relation to what the resulting observations can support. Increased clicks or completions alone do not establish better coverage, understanding, or answer quality. Nor does a changed answer distribution alone show which version is more accurate.

## Investigation

Compare meaningful candidates such as text-only, a functional icon, and a restrained illustration. Define what is being tested: comprehension, selection into participation, completion, answer interpretation, or another property. Where appropriate, consider randomized assignment and a stated analysis plan rather than inferring a causal effect from unrelated groups.

Preserve the exact image asset/version, placement, accompanying text, and assignment. Retain generation provenance where useful, but a prompt or model name is not a substitute for the image actually shown. Apply [Collection Context](../../Collection%20Context.md) without collecting unnecessary personal telemetry.

No illustration style, image-generation pipeline, or experiment is selected by this idea.
