# Knowledge and measurement foundations

Roughly's subject has substantial prior art in epistemology, measurement, survey statistics, and collective judgment. The useful task is to connect those traditions into precise definitions and an inspectable public resource. This first scoping review does not establish priority, exhaust the literature, or support claiming a newly discovered class of knowledge. Sources below were examined at the cited passages or sections; this is not a claim to have read every book in full. Connections to Roughly are interpretations unless explicitly attributed to an author.

## What is being defined

The working scope is **population proportions about human lives**: the share of a defined population satisfying a specified condition at a stated time or over a stated interval. Experiences, circumstances, actions, and preferences can all supply conditions. This is a tractable class of empirical targets, not a boundary around everything knowable. A precisely stated target can remain unobservable, poorly measured, vague at its edges, or dependent on a contested classification.

“Population proportion” supplies established mathematical language. “Estimand” identifies a target of estimation; “observed proportion” identifies a calculation on recorded evidence. “Proposition” identifies truth-evaluable content, whereas an open expression such as “has entered salt water” is a condition or predicate awaiting a subject. These roles are formalized in [Population Proportions](../Population%20Proportions.md). No new umbrella word is needed to begin the work.

It neither makes value judgments objective nor guarantees access to someone's experience. |

These are neighboring arguments, not one continuous doctrine that all authors accepted. Roughly can make modest methodological commitments without choosing a complete metaphysics or claiming to solve skepticism.

## Meaning and formalization

[Wittgenstein's Tractatus](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/5740/5740-pdf.pdf), 4.024, distinguishes understanding what would make a proposition true from knowing whether it is true. That is useful for separating a statement's definition from its measured value. His later [Philosophical Investigations](https://ericgerlach.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/3f786-ludwig.wittgenstein.-.philosophical.investigations.pdf), §43, ties meaning to use for a large class of cases, not all. This cautions against assuming that dictionary synonyms establish interchangeable survey instruments. These applications are Roughly's interpretations, not survey findings established by Wittgenstein.

[Carnap, Logical Foundations of Probability](https://www.fitelson.org/confirmation/carnap_logical_foundations_of_probability.pdf), chapter I, §§2–3, pp. 3–7 (PDF pages 28–32), supplies a direct methodological parallel: explication replaces an imprecise concept with a more exact one, assessed for similarity, exactness, fruitfulness, and simplicity. We can evaluate Roughly's definitions by worked examples and usefulness, without pretending to discover the one hidden name for the entire project. His distinction between frequency and confirmation also warns against treating a population share as a degree of belief in a claim. This does not adopt his full inductive logic.

[Gödel's 1931 incompleteness paper](https://www.uhu.es/francisco.moreno/gii_mac/docs/Godel_paper_1931.pdf), theorem VI, concerns formal systems meeting specific definability and consistency conditions. This linked partial translation uses modernized notation and includes translator commentary; the theorem is the relevant primary passage. Its application here is a boundary: formal unprovability, lack of empirical observations, ambiguous language, and practical inability to measure are different problems. The paper does not show that a particular human percentage is unknowable. No Gödel-based product principle is warranted by this review.

## Identification and the boundary of what data can establish

[Manski, Partial Identification of Probability Distributions](https://www.cemmap.ac.uk/wp-content/legacy/forms/manskipaper.pdf), introduction, pp. 1–5, directly addresses how data and assumptions constrain population parameters, sometimes to a set rather than one value. This is a close statistical counterpart to the project’s question about what evidence permits us to know. The [later identification paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.11318) separates identification using the observable distribution from finite-sample statistical analysis. It is not a theorem about all human knowledge. [Population Proportions](../Population%20Proportions.md) develops a bounded finite-population example and distinguishes identification regions from confidence intervals.

## Collective intelligence, uncertainty, and public understanding

[Galton's Vox Populi](https://gwern.net/doc/statistics/prediction/1907-galton.pdf), 1907, examines a particular crowd's estimates of an ox's weight. [James Surowiecki's The Wisdom of Crowds](https://www.asecib.ase.ro/mps/TheWisdomOfCrowds-JamesSurowiecki.pdf), chapter 1, p. 10 (PDF page 41), identifies diversity, independence, decentralization, and aggregation as conditions for wise crowds. Estimating a common external quantity differs from aggregating people's reports of their own experiences. Neither argument warrants saying that a majority preference is objectively better, or that a crowd necessarily describes the world's population.

[Lorenz, Rauhut, Schweitzer, and Helbing](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3107299/), 2011, found that social information could reduce estimate diversity without corresponding collective accuracy gains in their laboratory estimation tasks. That makes independence and prior exposure important research questions. It does not settle whether showing Roughly's results changes the accuracy of personal self-reports; collection-specific experiments remain necessary.

[Taleb's Black Swan glossary](https://www.fooledbyrandomness.com/glossary.pdf), entries on silent evidence, epistemic opacity, and the ludic fallacy, challenges what a visible record or tidy model leaves out. The practical connection is to exclusions, selection processes, and model assumptions. A bounded response indicator is not a financial loss with an unbounded tail; importing claims about heavy-tailed losses wholesale would be a mistake.

[Nate Silver's 2024 model-methodology update](https://www.natesilver.net/p/model-methodology-2024) exposes modeling choices and changes rather than presenting a forecast as a direct reading of reality. Roughly can similarly make analysis choices inspectable. This is a forecast-model example, not a review of all of The Signal and the Noise; descriptive shares, forecasts, and event probabilities must retain distinct meanings.

[Gapminder's Factfulness corrections](https://www.gapminder.org/factfulness-book/mistakes/) include a question-wording clarification and plans to test the revised wording. This is a useful public example of correcting explanations and instruments rather than treating a published number as final. It does not establish that Roughly should adopt quizzes or a particular editorial voice.

## Existing data infrastructures

[Data Commons](https://docs.datacommons.org/data_model.html) separates a statistical variable from observations at a time and place, with source and provenance. [W3C's RDF Data Cube](https://www.w3.org/TR/vocab-data-cube/) represents observations with dimensions, measures, and attributes. These are close engineering precedents for a reusable body of statistical knowledge, not epistemologies or required dependencies. Roughly still needs exact question identity, response meaning, collection context, and explicit population predicates.

## Decisions this review supports

Use established terms before coining a brand-specific noun. Keep definitions, targets, observations, methods, and prose renderings separate. Preserve exact instruments and explicit relations between them. Describe a recorded sample directly, and attach an inference method before extending a result to another population. Treat unknown, undefined, withheld, and zero as different states. Make revisions and evidence inspectable without implying that reproducibility establishes external truth.

These commitments feed [Population Proportions](../Population%20Proportions.md), [Claims and Evidence](../Claims%20and%20Evidence.md), and [the answer-statement proposal](2026-11-03-answer-statements.md). They do not implement that proposal or resolve public collection policy.

## Further reading and unresolved research

The next deeper investigations should compare alternative accounts rather than add names to an endorsement list: Bayesian inference and calibration; Condorcet's jury assumptions versus correlated judgments; Popper on testing versus description; Quine on theory and observation; classification and social construction in statistics; non-Western accounts of perception, inference, and testimony; and public statistical communication beyond election forecasting. The present review centers on accessible Western philosophical and statistical sources and cannot stand in for those investigations.

A novelty claim would require a much more systematic literature review, including philosophy of social science, formal ontology, and existing statistical knowledge graphs. A useful synthesis, careful public definitions, and open software are worthwhile contributions without that claim. [The publication plan](2026-11-03-guide-and-essays.md) separates living explanations from a possible dated essay series.
