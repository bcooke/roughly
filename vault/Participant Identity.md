# Participant Identity

Public attribution, operator access to identity, continuity of participation, proof of personhood, and verified legal identity are separate dimensions. A pseudonym can hide a name from readers while leaving responses identifiable to the operator.

An account can support continuity and one-response-per-account controls. It does not establish one person per account or truthful answers. Stronger verification changes participation friction, inclusion, privacy exposure, and trust as well as duplicate-participant controls. Its effect on honesty is not a premise to assume.

Describe actual linkage, access, retention, and erasure behavior when making privacy assurances. [Domain Writes](Domain%20Writes.md) explains why event authority does not settle storage of sensitive personal values. [Polling Methodology](Polling%20Methodology.md) separates integrity controls from population inference.

Policy alternatives and evidence needs belong in [the participation investigation](discovery/2026-11-02-participation-design.md) and [R-002](delivery/R-002-participation-methodology.md). This note does not prescribe accounts, anonymity, or identity-document collection.
