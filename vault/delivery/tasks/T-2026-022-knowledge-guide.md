# Knowledge foundations and public guide

## Outcome

Research related ideas across philosophy, language, logic, measurement, sampling, and collective intelligence. Define a finite-population working scope and shared vocabulary without claiming a new theory or collapsing targets into observations. Give readers a maintained public Guide, with definitions, an explanation of knowledge and evidence, and a grouped source map.

[Knowledge and measurement foundations](../../discovery/2026-11-03-knowledge-and-measurement.md) retains source passages, interpretations, and limits. [Population Proportions](../../Population%20Proportions.md) owns the formal definitions. [Guide and essays](../../discovery/2026-11-03-guide-and-essays.md) separates evergreen explanations from a possible dated series.

No semantic domain events, sentence-template contract, public collection, or essay publishing system are introduced. The guide points to the existing repository contribution workflow and does not imply public data submission is available.
