# Investigate participation and data quality

## Question

How can Roughly support free exploration while reducing distortions introduced by the collection experience?

## Acceptance criteria

Produce a reviewed proposal that distinguishes results exposure, answer gating, predictions, public anonymity, operator identity access, account continuity, duplicate controls, and stronger identity assurance. Cover privacy, inclusion, honesty, withdrawal, and personal values in event history. Identify evidence needs and competing effects; a changed distribution or higher engagement is not proof of greater truthfulness.

No mandatory-account, KYC, or answer-to-unlock policy is assumed. This work does not authorize collecting responses or identity documents.
