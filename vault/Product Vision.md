# Product Vision

Roughly is an open collection of the world's percentages: human preferences, behavior, and attitudes made explorable through questions. Its purpose is to make measurable facts about human lives easier to investigate and find. These include experiences and actions as well as preferences. [Quantifiable Human Facts](Quantifiable%20Human%20Facts.md) defines that scope, and [Claims and Evidence](Claims%20and%20Evidence.md) separates the quantity of interest from what a source establishes.

Free access, open source, transparent methods, and independence from a profit incentive guide product choices. Nonprofit governance is a possibility to evaluate, not a claim about an established organization.

A percentage needs its question, sample, and interpretation. [Polling Methodology](Polling%20Methodology.md) defines that responsibility. [Results Exposure](Results%20Exposure.md) explains why an exploration experience and a response-collection experience cannot be assumed to have identical requirements.

Questions can support conversation and writing through [Question Links](Question%20Links.md). [Design Philosophy](Design%20Philosophy.md) places data and context ahead of interface ornament.
